# Product

## Register

product

## Users

School administrators completing the Heritage Pro onboarding wizard for their institution. They use the tool to provide implementation information in stages, often returning later to continue an incomplete setup.

## Product Purpose

The onboarding wizard collects the institution, academic, operational, migration, integration, finance, and evidence information needed to configure Heritage Pro. Success means school administrators can understand what each field expects, save partial work safely, and complete the required setup without specialist technical knowledge.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid unexplained technical jargon, ambiguous blank fields, dense forms without progressive disclosure, and interfaces that make users guess whether information is required or how precise an answer should be.

## Design Principles

- Explain what each field expects in plain language.
- Make required work obvious and optional work easy to defer.
- Preserve progress and support confident return visits.
- Reveal complexity progressively so administrators can focus on the current decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use plain language, visible labels, meaningful placeholder examples, keyboard-accessible controls, clear required/optional states, and validation feedback that does not rely on color alone. Support reduced motion and maintain accessible focus and error announcement behavior.
